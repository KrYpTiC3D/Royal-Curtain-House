Our Story

From Local Workshop to Regional Powerhouse

Founded in 1998, Royal Curtain House began with a commitment to bring high-end architectural drapery to Sri Lankan homeowners with honest, direct-from-importer craftsmanship and exceptional value.

Over 25 years, our footprint has expanded strategically across three key provincial hubs: Kekirawa on the vital A9 Kandy-Jaffna Highway, Katugastota serving the affluent Kandy District and Central Hills, and Matale on Trincomalee Street.

Unlike ordinary retailers who purchase from local middlemen, we directly import specialized ripple-fold tapes, heavy-gauge fabrics, and motorized track systems. This allows us to guarantee uncompromising craftsmanship, flawless ceiling-recessed wave drapes, and unbeatable value.
