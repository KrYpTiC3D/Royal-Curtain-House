## Users
- **Broad Residential Clients:** Homeowners, luxury villa proprietors (coastal villas in Galle, hill-country residences in Kandy and Nuwara Eliya, luxury apartments in Colombo) seeking custom architectural window treatments tailored to exact floor-to-ceiling dimensions.
- **Commercial & Hospitality Clients:** Boutique hotels, corporate offices, dining venues, and retail spaces across Sri Lanka requiring heavy-duty, motorized, or calibrated light-control drapery and blinds.
- **Architects & Interior Designers:** Design professionals seeking direct master workshop collaboration, exact pleat fullness ratios (e.g., 2.0x–2.4x ripple fold wave), ceiling recess integration details, and physical textile swatches for on-site client presentations.

## Product Purpose
To showcase and deliver bespoke architectural window treatments—specifically engineered ripple fold wave drapery, day & night zebra blinds, 100% thermal/acoustic blackout systems, and smart motorized tracks—crafted in the Matale master workshop and deployed with complimentary digital laser measurement across all nine provinces of Sri Lanka. Success means enabling clients and designers to size, configure, and effortlessly initiate high-touch bespoke orders through WhatsApp and showroom engagement.

## Positioning
Unlike generic curtain retailers supplying pre-stitched or mass-market window coverings, Royal Curtain House combines over two decades of artisanal Matale tailoring (est. 1998; double-fold 4-inch bottom hems, hand-inserted drapery weights, invisible blind stitching) with island-wide complimentary laser measurement and on-site fabric swatch evaluation anywhere in Sri Lanka.

## Operating Context
- **Customer Discovery & Sizing:** Exploring collections online, calculating custom window specifications via the interactive sizing engine, and scheduling on-site appointments via WhatsApp or visiting the Matale flagship showroom.
- **On-Site Measurement & Swatch Review:** Specialists travel to private villas, apartments, or commercial sites across Sri Lanka with laser measurement devices and full swatch books (500+ direct-imported architectural fabrics).
- **Fabrication & Installation:** Custom fabrication in the Matale workshop followed by dust-free, clean on-site installation of silent-glide concealed tracks, motorized systems, and weighted drapery panels.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Digital Sizing Engine:** Interactive curtain sizing and specification tool calculating fabric yardage (meters), track span (inches), panel count, fullness ratios (Wave 2.2x, French Pleat 2.6x, Eyelet 1.8x, Minimal 1.5x), and floor drops (kissing, hovering, gentle break, dramatic puddle) with immediate WhatsApp inquiry dispatch.
- **Technical Capabilities:** Smart motorization support (smartphone/automation control), concealed ceiling-recess tracks, thermal defense blackout, variable-tilt zebra cassette systems.
- **Technical Stack & Constraints:** Static HTML5, modular CSS, vanilla JavaScript, GSAP, Lenis smooth scrolling, Three.js liquid canvas effect, and Playwright automated testing. Lightweight client-side architecture without heavyweight backend frameworks.
- **Geographic & Fulfillment Scope:** Flagship showroom at No 553, Trincomalee Street, Matale 21000; free on-site consultations and measurements offered across all 9 provinces of Sri Lanka.

## Product Principles
1. **Precision Before Production:** Never estimate loosely—accurate laser measurements, ceiling recess clearance checks, and exact fullness ratios govern every custom installation.
2. **Artisanal Craftsmanship, Modern Engineering:** Pair classic tailoring methods (weighted hems, blind stitching) with cutting-edge silent motorized tracks and home automation compatibility.
3. **Frictionless Island-Wide Access:** High-end architectural window design made accessible across all 9 provinces through doorstep consultation and frictionless WhatsApp communication.
4. **Authenticity in Material & Mechanism:** Represent textile behavior, fullness, and light filtration truthfully; let real fabric quality lead every design surface.
