End-to-End Execution

Island-Wide Window Treatment Services

From laser measurements at your doorstep anywhere in Sri Lanka to master workshop tailoring in Matale and dust-free professional installation.

01
Phase One

Island-Wide On-Site Measurement & Consultation

Our architectural drapery consultants visit your property anywhere in Sri Lanka—from private villas in Galle to luxury apartments in Colombo and hill-country estates in Kandy and Nuwara Eliya.

Using precision digital laser measures, we assess frame depth, ceiling recess clearance, stack-back space, and sun exposure angles. We bring comprehensive swatch books containing over 500+ direct-imported fabrics so you can evaluate textures under your room's natural lighting.

Laser Precision Full Fabric Swatch Books All 9 Provinces Covered
02
Phase Two

Master Tailoring in Our Matale Workshop

Every drapery order is tailored in our dedicated Matale master facility by artisans with over two decades of technical experience.

We employ double-fold 4-inch bottom hems, hand-inserted drapery weights, invisible blind-stitching, and exact carrier spacing. Whether crafting ripple fold waves with calibrated fullness or triple pinch pleats, every panel hangs with fluid architectural uniformity.

Handcrafted in Matale Weighted Hems Direct Fabric Imports
03
Phase Three

Smart Motorization & Concealed Tracks

For contemporary smart homes and expansive high-ceiling double-height windows, we configure ultra-silent motorized tracks and automated roller/zebra cassettes.

Systems support rechargeable lithium-battery motors, hardwired line power, handheld multi-channel remotes, and direct smart home integration with Apple HomeKit, Alexa, Google Home, and Tuya.

Silent Motors (< 30dB) App & Voice Automation Multi-Channel Remotes
04
Phase Four

Turnkey Installation & Steam Dressing

Our specialized installation crew arrives on site with dust-free containment equipment, commercial steamers, and structural anchors suitable for concrete, drywall, and timber soffits.

After structural mounting, curtains are professionally dressed and memory-steamed on the track to lock in pleat memory, leaving your space clean, pristine, and ready to enjoy.

Dust-Free Mounting Memory Steam Dressing Full Warranty Support
