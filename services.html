Comprehensive Offerings

The Royal Standard of Service

A seamless journey from concept and fabric selection to finished architectural installation.

Free On-Site Laser Measurement

Technicians from our Katugastota, Kekirawa, or Matale branches visit your home or business to take precision measurements with full fabric swatches — completely free of charge.

Design & Textile Consultation

Work directly with our window fashion specialists to balance light filtration, privacy, and thermal cooling for your specific architectural floor plan.

Bespoke In-House Tailoring

Crafted in our dedicated workshop with weighted corner corners, precision wave spacing, and double-turned hems to guarantee perfectly uniform folds.

Professional Track & Rod Fitting

Our experienced fitters mount ceiling-recessed ripple tracks, brass rods, and outdoor monsoon brackets securely, leaving your interior spotless.

Smart Home Motorization

Upgrade to motorized curtains and zebra blinds with silent DC motors, wireless wall switches, smartphone app automation, and voice control via Google Assistant & Apple Home.

5-Year Workmanship Guarantee

We stand firmly behind our craft. Every bespoke tailoring job, track mechanism, and motor system is backed by our comprehensive 5-year guarantee.
